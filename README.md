# Plate-meal-planner
Plate is a simple, friendly app for anyone who wants to eat better and keep up with the gym, without spending hours on it. Make Plate yours with light and dark mode and colour themes. Your data is tied to your own account, and it works on your phone or laptop and syncs between them.  Plate is in early beta, so your feedback shapes what comes next.
